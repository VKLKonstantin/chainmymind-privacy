ChainMyMind — Privacy Policy

Dated: 13 October 2026

This data privacy, use and retention policy (the “Policy”) applies to all processing of information in the ChainMyMind app and on the app website carried out by the developer of ChainMyMind (Konstantin) (“we”, “us”, “our”).

The Policy explains what information the app processes, where it comes from, who receives it, how long it is kept and what rights you have. By using the app you confirm that you have read this Policy.

1. Who we are

ChainMyMind is a private encrypted journal. Notes are encrypted on your device and written to a smart contract on the public Polygon network. The app has no servers of its own, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers.

We do not receive, store or sell your data. Processing is limited to the information the app cannot work without.

2. The information we process

The app processes the following information:

(a) Notes. The text is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it is sent. The encryption key is derived from your wallet signature and never leaves the device. Only the encrypted text reaches the blockchain.

(b) Wallet address. Used to find your notes and show your balance. When you save a note, the address is publicly linked to the transaction.

(c) Private key and seed phrase. When you sign in with a key, it is used once on the device to create a signature. The app neither stores nor sends it anywhere.

(d) Voice. The microphone turns on only when you tap the dictation button and only with your permission. Speech is recognized by your system or browser service (for example, Google on Android or Apple on iOS) under its own terms. The app receives only the recognized text and does not keep any audio.

(e) Technical data. The services listed in section 5 see your IP address and the content of the request. We do not receive this data.

3. How we obtain information

Information comes from you and is processed on your device: you write or dictate a note, connect a wallet or enter a key.

The app reads your saved notes from the public Polygon blockchain by your wallet address. We do not obtain information about you from any other source.

4. How we use information

Information is used only for the features you start yourself:

(a) encrypting, saving, reading and exporting notes;

(b) connecting a wallet, signing messages and sending transactions that you confirm in your wallet;

(c) showing your address, balance and the POL price;

(d) converting speech to text on your command.

The basis for processing is providing the app features you request. Microphone access is based on your consent, which you can withdraw in your device settings. We do not use information for advertising, marketing, profiling or automated decision-making.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your information. The app contacts the services below directly from your device, and they handle requests under their own terms:

(a) Reown (WalletConnect) — connecting to your wallet, relaying signature requests and reading Polygon network data. Messages between the app and the wallet are encrypted.

(b) Polygon network nodes (dRPC and Reown) — reading notes and balances and sending signed transactions.

(c) CoinGecko — the POL price on the wallet screen. Only the price request is sent.

(d) Your wallet (for example, Trust Wallet or MetaMask) — operates under its own privacy policy.

(e) Polygonscan — opens only if you follow a link to a transaction or address yourself.

6. The public blockchain

The Polygon blockchain is public. Anyone can see the wallet address, the encrypted entry, its hash, the date and the transaction fee. The text cannot be read without your key.

Entries on the blockchain cannot be changed or deleted — neither by you nor by us. Keep this in mind before saving a note.

7. Information on your device

The app stores your wallet address, sign-in method, language, settings and wallet connection session data. It also keeps the signature used to decrypt notes, unless “Require wallet signature to view” is turned on.

This data is excluded from Android backups and device transfers. Signing out removes the sign-in data and decryption keys; uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes everything else.

8. Transferring information overseas

The services in section 5 and the Polygon network nodes may be located in different countries, including outside your country of residence. Requests from your device reach them only to run the features you use.

9. How long we keep information

We do not keep your information: we have no servers or databases.

(a) Data on your device is kept until you sign out, uninstall the app or clear site data (section 7).

(b) Encrypted notes stay on the Polygon blockchain permanently and cannot be deleted (section 6).

(c) Third-party services keep request data under their own terms.

10. Security

Notes are encrypted on the device before they are sent, and the encryption key never leaves the device. Requests travel over secure connections (HTTPS and WSS). App data is excluded from Android backups.

When “Require wallet signature to view” is on, the decryption key is kept in memory only. Do not enter a private key or seed phrase on a device you do not trust.

11. Your rights

Depending on the laws of your country, you may have the rights below. All information stays with you, so you exercise most of them yourself in the app:

(a) Access and portability — view your notes in the app and export them to JSON, TXT or PDF.

(b) Erasure — sign out, uninstall the app or clear site data. Entries on the blockchain cannot be deleted.

(c) Rectification — a saved note cannot be changed, but you can save a new one.

(d) Withdrawal of consent — deny microphone access in your device settings. All other features keep working.

(e) Objection and restriction — stop using a feature or uninstall the app.

(f) Complaint — contact the data protection authority in your country.

Send questions about your rights to cryptowalletvkl@gmail.com. We hold no data linked to your identity, so we cannot find or delete it on request.

12. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly process their data.

13. Changes to this Policy

A new version of the Policy is published on this page with its effective date. If the changes affect how data is processed, we will let you know in the app.

14. How to contact us

For questions about privacy or this Policy, write to cryptowalletvkl@gmail.com.
